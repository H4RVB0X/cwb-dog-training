Every dog has a superpower: their nose. Scentwork gives that superpower a job, and the results go far beyond a few fun sessions on a Sunday morning.

## A tired mind is a happy dog

A mentally stimulated dog is usually a happier dog. Searching for a scent makes your dog think, problem-solve and concentrate, which can tire them out in a way a long walk doesn't.

## It builds confidence and calm

Every successful find gives your dog a real confidence boost. Sniffing is naturally calming, and having a clear task gives dogs a sense of purpose. It's a great outlet for energy that can help prevent unwanted behaviours at home.

## You become a team

Working together on a fun, rewarding task strengthens your bond. You learn to read your dog, and your dog learns that you're the most interesting thing in the field.

## Any dog can do it

You don't need any experience, and age is no barrier. Dogs from 7 months to 13 years old have taken part in our UK Sniffer Dogs courses so far. The Bronze course covers:

- **Scent identification:** teaching your dog the target scent
- **Searching skills:** how to search an area methodically
- **Scent indication:** how your dog tells you they've found it

Courses run outdoors in Wednesbury, which makes them ideal for dogs who need a little more room.
